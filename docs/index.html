What this adds to Horilla

Horilla's HR modules stay as they are. This fork extends the Recruitment module so hiring runs largely on its own.

Gmail & Outlook integration

Connect a mailbox with OAuth. Read applications from it, and optionally send HR mail through your Outlook account instead of SMTP.

Automatic candidate intake

A candidate emails a PDF resume, and a record appears in the right job's pipeline. The mailbox is polled every 5 minutes and every email is logged.

AI resume scoring

A 0-100 score with key points, reasoning and a confidence level for every new candidate with a resume. Includes checks against hallucinated skills and wrong-resume uploads.

Better-job match recommender

Tells you when a candidate fits another open job better, and moves them there in one click.

Teams & Google Meet interviews

Scheduling an interview creates the calendar event and meeting link, and mails the interviewers and the candidate. Reschedules update the same event.

Online and offline interviews

Offline interviews are still on the calendar and mailed, just without a video link. The end time is optional.

How it works

From an email in the inbox to a scheduled interview.

  1. A candidate emails an application with a PDF resume to the connected Gmail or Outlook mailbox.
  2. The intake poller reads it, and the AI extracts the candidate's details and picks the matching open job.
  3. The candidate is created in that job's Applied stage, and duplicates are detected.
  4. AI scoring rates the resume against the job, then the match check compares it with the other open jobs.
  5. A better-fit job shows as a badge on the pipeline card, with a one-click "Move to suggested job".
  6. Schedule the interview, and a Teams or Google Meet event is created and mailed to everyone.

Free to run

AI runs on a local Ollama model at no cost, or on OpenAI (pay per use). The repository includes a complete $0 production setup on Oracle Cloud Always Free: Django on ASGI, PostgreSQL, Redis, nginx and Caddy with automatic HTTPS.

FAQ

Is this the official Horilla HRMS?

No. It is an independent fork of the open-source Horilla HRMS that extends the Recruitment module. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Horilla.

Does it need a paid AI service?

No. AI features can run on a local Ollama model on your own server for free, or on OpenAI, which is pay per use. Choose in Settings, Integrations, AI Settings.

Which email providers does candidate intake support?

Gmail (Google OAuth) and Outlook (Microsoft Graph). The mailbox is polled every 5 minutes and PDF resumes are turned into candidates.

Which video meeting tools are supported for interviews?

Microsoft Teams and Google Meet. Offline interviews are still added to the calendar and mailed, without a video link.
