Module 01 · Gmail & Outlook

Applications arrive. Candidates appear.

Connect a Gmail or Outlook mailbox. The poller reads new applications, the AI pulls the candidate's details out of the PDF resume and picks the matching open job, and a candidate lands in that job's Applied stage.

  • Gmail (Google OAuth) and Outlook (Microsoft Graph)
  • Mailbox polled every 5 minutes, every email logged, duplicates caught
  • Outlook can also send your HR mail instead of SMTP

Module 02 · AI scoring

Every resume, scored and explained.

Each new candidate with a resume gets a 0-100 score, key points, the reasoning and a confidence level, automatically and in the background.

  • Two scoring passes, averaged. Disagreement lowers the confidence
  • "Missing skill" claims are checked against the resume text
  • Wrong resume attached? Score capped at 50, with a warning
Ollama (free, local)OpenAIHistory kept
Module 03 · Better-job match

A better-fitting role, found for you.

After scoring, the resume is compared with your other open jobs. If one beats the current job by 15 or more points (or reaches 70 when there is no score yet), a badge appears on the candidate's card with a one-click Move to suggested job.

  • Compares against up to 15 other open jobs
  • A small gap is treated as noise, so you are not spammed

The numbers in the 3D scene are an illustration.

Module 04 · Teams & Google Meet

Schedule once. Everyone gets the link.

Choose Microsoft Teams or Google Meet per company. Scheduling an interview creates the calendar event and meeting link and mails the interviewers and the candidate.

  • Online and offline modes. Offline stays on the calendar, without a video link
  • Reschedules update the same event. Deleting marks the invite cancelled first
  • A meeting-link problem never blocks scheduling

FAQ

Is this the official Horilla HRMS?

No. It is an independent fork of the open-source Horilla HRMS that extends the Recruitment module. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Horilla.

Does it need a paid AI service?

No. AI features can run on a local Ollama model on your own server for free, or on OpenAI, which is pay per use. Choose in Settings, Integrations, AI Settings.

Which email providers does candidate intake support?

Gmail (Google OAuth) and Outlook (Microsoft Graph). The mailbox is polled every 5 minutes and PDF resumes are turned into candidates.

Which video meeting tools are supported for interviews?

Microsoft Teams and Google Meet. Offline interviews are still added to the calendar and mailed, without a video link.
